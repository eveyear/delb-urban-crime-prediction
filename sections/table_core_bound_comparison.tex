Continuous entropy prediction bound \citep{fang_variance_bounds}
& Real-valued target; squared error
& Conditional differential entropy mapped through the Gaussian
entropy--variance relation
& Equality requires an innovation with the appropriate Gaussian form and
independence properties
& Continuous analogue; it does not supply the exact integer-lattice
envelope or its lattice correction\\

Massey lattice bound \citep{massey_integer_entropy,rioul_massey}
& Integer-valued variable; variance or second moment
& Discrete maximum entropy and the conservative \(1/12\)-corrected
closed form
& Characterizes the lattice extremizer, but does not by itself specify a
causal predictor, its history-dependent refinement, or side-information
ordering
& Supplies established lattice ingredients; neither the extremizer nor the
closed-form inequality is claimed as new\\

Fano inequality \citep{cover_thomas,feder_merhav}
& Finite-alphabet target; Hamming loss
& Conditional entropy related to classification-error probability
& Concerns the optimal classification decision on the appropriate inverse
branch
& A complementary loss-specific check; it is not an MSE bound and is not
part of the DELB novelty claim\\

General Bayes-risk and rate--distortion bounds
\citep{shannon_rate_distortion,chen_bayes_risk}
& General estimand and specified loss or distortion
& Minimum decision risk or operational distortion, often through
hypothesis testing or coding arguments
& Defined through optimization over a decision class; tightness depends on
the particular bound and statistical experiment
& Broader than DELB. DELB is an explicit information-theoretic lower bound
on integer-valued squared-error risk, not Bayes risk itself\\

Present DELB package
& Causal integer-valued target; squared error
& Exact inverse lattice envelope, with a predictor-dependent refinement
& A given predictor attains the universal bound exactly when its error is
moment-matched discrete Gaussian and independent of the admissible history;
equality with optimal risk also requires an optimizing predictor to exist
& Adds the two-slack diagnostic, CMI ordering for new information, and a
finite-sample estimation--randomization workflow; it is a synthesis rather
than a new primitive entropy inequality\\
