\subsection{Experiment Lineage}
\label{app:experiment_lineage}

The empirical results were produced sequentially:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=2.4em]
    \item E01 standardized crime events and bicycle trips, resolved endpoint
    coordinates, and retained reason-coded exclusions;
    \item E02 constructed the leakage-safe 1~km daily panels and descriptive
    figures;
    \item E03 numerically checked the exact integer entropy envelope and its numerical
    inverse;
    \item E04 estimated city-level conditional information and DELB;
    \item E05 estimated local information value and spatial clustering;
    \item E06 fitted matched out-of-sample integer predictors;
    \item E07 formed predictability gaps and local theory--practice
    comparisons;
    \item E08 repeated theory and prediction by harmonized crime category;
    \item E09 evaluated pre-specified theory and rolling-origin robustness;
    \item E10 calibrated city and local statistics against three null
    designs and compared recent with distant mobility lags;
    \item E11 synthesized accepted E02 and E04--E10 outputs into tables,
    figures, and macros for Version 1;
    \item E12 simulated plug-in and Miller--Madow CMI under known
    population truth and E10-style randomization designs;
    \item E13 reconstructed every E10 surrogate and recorded its support
    atoms, singleton occupancy, and effective degrees of freedom;
    \item E14 evaluated oracle and empirical binary and three-level Fano
    floors;
    \item E15 generated the estimand--estimator--randomization conceptual
    figure in single- and double-column layouts;
    \item S7 read only the accepted E12--E15 outputs, generated LaTeX tables
    and macros, copied figures after SHA-256 verification, and integrated
    Version 2;
    \item E16 constructed nested 500~m, 1~km, and 2~km daily and weekly
    panels, generated frozen block-thinning indices, estimated DELBs and
    support diagnostics across five sample fractions, applied area--time
    normalization, and calibrated all 90 scale--sample cells against three
    randomization mechanisms; S16.7 generated the manuscript macros and
    tables and copied 12 figure files after SHA-256 verification;
    \item E17 applied plugin, Miller--Madow, and observed-support
    Jeffreys--Dirichlet estimators to the same 999 city-level surrogates,
    reported false-discovery-rate adjustment within each estimator's nine
    tests and globally across all 27 tests, and summarized the
    training-period-frozen analysis-domain sensitivity;
    \item E18 evaluated a baseline-state-matched restricted randomization
    with 999 city-level replicates and assessed its Type I error and power
    on empirical-support semi-synthetic panels.
\end{enumerate}

All accepted stages passed their registered row, schema, identity, ordering,
and artifact checks. The final S7 synthesis verified four source-run
pointers, four generated LaTeX files, seven hash-identical source figures,
six Fano city--target rows, 64 Fano oracle rows, and zero estimated Fano
violations.

\subsection{Robustness and Null-Calibration Scope}
\label{app:robustness_scope}

E09 contains 144 theory rows (16 specifications, three estimators, and
three cities), 144 monthly rolling model--origin results, and three
bootstrap block lengths per city. E10 contains 8991 city-level null
replicates, 1,015,983 local null replicates, 339 joint local screens, and
matched 14- and 30-day lag diagnostics. These artifacts are retained in
their accepted run directories. The null designs are treated
as finite-sample diagnostics rather than causal or exact conditional
randomization tests. Every article figure, including the E11 workflow, was
created by a versioned Python entry point and was not manually edited.
E17 contains 26,973 estimator-specific city-level null statistics
(8991 per estimator); its Miller--Madow statistics reproduce E10 exactly.
E18 contains 2997 empirical city-level restricted-randomization replicates
and 2400 semi-synthetic dataset-level calibration summaries.

Descriptive figures, estimator sensitivity, training-period-frozen
analysis-domain sensitivity, and the extended local, multiscale,
crime-type, rolling-origin, and Fano diagnostics are supplied in the
independent Supplementary Materials file.
