\subsection{Reproducible Analysis Components}
\label{app:experiment_lineage}

The reproducible analysis comprises data harmonization, leakage-safe panel
construction, numerical verification of the integer entropy envelope,
city-level and local DELB estimation, matched held-out prediction,
crime-category and rolling-origin analyses, randomization calibration,
known-truth simulation, support diagnostics, Fano comparisons, multiscale
evaluation, estimator sensitivity, and baseline-state-matched power
analysis. Version-controlled scripts preserve the configurations, random
seeds, reason-coded exclusions, intermediate checks, and machine-readable
outputs for each component.

\subsection{Robustness and Null-Calibration Scope}
\label{app:robustness_scope}

The robustness analysis contains 144 theory rows (16 specifications, three
estimators, and three cities), 144 monthly rolling model--origin results, and
three bootstrap block lengths per city. The randomization analysis contains
8,991 city-level reference
replicates, 1,015,983 local null replicates, 339 joint local screens, and
matched 14- and 30-day lag diagnostics. These artifacts are retained in
their accepted run directories. The null designs are treated
as finite-sample diagnostics rather than causal or exact conditional
randomization tests. Every article figure was created by a versioned Python
entry point and was not manually edited. Estimator sensitivity comprises
26,973 city-level reference statistics (8,991 per estimator), with identical
Miller--Madow statistics in the primary and sensitivity analyses. The
restricted-randomization analysis contains 2,997 empirical city-level
replicates and 2,400 semi-synthetic calibration summaries. The scale--sample
analysis retains 12 fixed-design panels and 9,600 generated datasets, together
with truth, power, minimum-detectable-CMI, and support summaries.

Descriptive figures, estimator sensitivity, training-period-frozen
decision-population comparison, and the extended local, multiscale,
crime-type, rolling-origin, and Fano diagnostics are supplied in the
independent Supplementary Materials file.
